Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian pasta dish made of wide, flat pasta sheets layered with fillings like ragù (meat sauce), béchamel or ricotta cheese, and melted mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • Pasta sheets
  • Meat
  • Cheese
  • Tomato sauce

Steps

  1. Cook the ground meat, onion, and garlic in a skillet until browned, then stir in the crushed tomatoes and tomato paste to create the meat sauce.
  2. Boil the lasagna noodles in a pot of salted water until al dente, then drain them.
  3. Mix the ricotta cheese, parmesan, egg, and parsley together in a bowl.
  4. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  5. Assemble the lasagna in a baking dish by layering meat sauce, noodles, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella cheese.
  6. Repeat the layers until the dish is filled, topping the final layer with extra mozzarella and parmesan cheese.
  7. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake for another 15 minutes until bubbly and golden brown.
  8. Let it cool for 15 minutes before slicing and serving.
