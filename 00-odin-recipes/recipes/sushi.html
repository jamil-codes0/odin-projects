Sushi

Description

Sushi is a traditional Japanese dish made of seasoned vinegared rice, typically paired with raw or cooked seafood, vegetables, and sometimes tropical fruits.

Ingredients

  • Fish
  • Rice
  • Seaweed

Steps

  1. Prepare the sushi rice – Wash short-grain Japanese rice thoroughly, cook it, and gently fold in a mixture of rice vinegar, sugar, and salt while it cools.
  2. Prep the fillings – Slice your sushi-grade fish, vegetables (like cucumber and avocado), and other proteins into long, thin strips.
  3. Set up your station – Place a sheet of nori (shiny side down) on a bamboo rolling mat wrapped in plastic wrap, and keep a bowl of water nearby to wet your hands.
  4. Spread the rice – Wet your fingers and evenly press a thin layer of rice across the nori, leaving a small border at the top edge.
  5. Layer the ingredients – Arrange your sliced fish and vegetables horizontally in a neat line across the center of the rice.
  6. Roll the sushi – Lift the edge of the bamboo mat closest to you, roll it tightly over the fillings, and squeeze gently to shape it into a firm cylinder.
  7. Slice into pieces – Use a very sharp knife wiped with a damp cloth to slice the roll cleanly into bite-sized rounds.
  8. Serve immediately – Arrange the pieces on a platter and serve with soy sauce, wasabi, and pickled ginger.
