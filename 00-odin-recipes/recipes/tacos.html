Tacos

Description

A taco is a traditional Mexican dish consisting of a small, hand-sized corn or wheat tortilla topped with a filling.

Ingredients

  • chicken
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Corn tortilla

Steps

  1. Prepare the protein: Dice your meat (like flank steak or chicken) into small, bite-sized pieces and season generously with cumin, chili powder, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  2. Cook the filling: Heat oil in a heavy skillet over high heat, add the meat, and sear until it is fully cooked and slightly charred at the edges.
  3. Warm the tortillas: Heat a dry skillet over medium-high heat and warm each corn tortilla for about 30 seconds per side until soft and pliable.
  4. Assemble the base: Place two warm tortillas on top of each other for strength, then spoon a generous portion of the cooked meat into the center.
  5. Add the garnishes: Top the meat with finely diced white onions and fresh chopped cilantro.
  6. Finish and serve: Squeeze fresh lime juice over the top, drizzle with your favorite salsa, and serve immediately while hot.
